## User Roles & What They Can Do

| Role | Capabilities |
|------|-------------|
| `traveller` | View own trip (published only), submit feedback/survey, update own profile & documents |
| `leader` | All traveller abilities + view all members, itinerary, contacts for **their assigned trips** |
| `admin` | Full access to everything — creates users, trips, itinerary, publishes trips |
